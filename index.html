About Me

I'm a Full Stack Software Engineer and the founder of Ajobaba Technologies. For over five years I've designed, built and shipped products used by students, companies and everyday consumers, from SaaS platforms for HR teams to mobile apps on the Google Play Store.

I started Ajobaba Technologies to give businesses a dependable technology partner: one team that understands both the code and the commercial goals behind it. I oversee every project from the first consultation through to launch and ongoing support.
